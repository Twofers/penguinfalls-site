About Penguin Falls

Penguin Falls LLC was founded by a Coast Guard veteran and SMU Cox MBA graduate. The company builds practical software that helps local businesses connect with customers, improve operations, and create measurable value.

The company builds and operates its own products, beginning with Twofer.

Our First Product

Twofer

Twofer helps coffee shops, cafés, bakeries, and other local merchants run limited-time buy-one-get-one offers that bring customers in during slower periods.

Twofer focuses on simple offer creation, live local discovery, and frictionless QR-based redemption.
