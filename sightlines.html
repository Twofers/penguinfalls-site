Three ways to explore.

Nearby

Explore likely addresses and roads with a directional map cone. Adjust your range and angle, then review candidates with confidence information.

Night Mode

Use a low-light view to explore a direction and create a report. Successful scans are saved on your device to revisit later.

World Line

Follow a line toward cities, countries, oceans, and landmarks. The bundled geographic dataset works offline without a subscription.

Sightlines is for discovery. GPS, compass calibration, and map data affect accuracy. See Sightlines support for scan allowances, Plus subscription details, and help getting started.
