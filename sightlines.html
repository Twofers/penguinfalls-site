Sightlines: Direction Finder

Follow your curiosity.

Ever wonder what lies in that direction? Sightlines pairs your phone's location and compass with a map you can explore. Adjust the distance and angle, discover nearby candidates, or follow World Line toward places farther away.

iPhone · Free download · Optional Plus subscription

What you can do with Sightlines

01

Explore nearby

Use a map cone to find likely addresses and roads. Results include confidence information so you can understand their limits.

02

Look a little farther

World Line explores cities, countries, oceans, and landmarks with a bundled geographic dataset available offline.

03

Keep your discoveries

Revisit saved reports on your device, or use Night Mode's low-light interface for a fresh cloud scan.
