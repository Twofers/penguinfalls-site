Twofer

Twofer helps local merchants create controlled, limited-time offers that can bring nearby customers into the store during slower periods. The platform combines AI-assisted offer creation, live discovery, and QR-based redemption.

Visit Twofer →

Business features

  • Create offers quickly
  • Control when offers appear
  • Set limited availability
  • Redeem in store with QR codes
  • Test demand during slower hours

Consumer features

  • Discover nearby live specials
  • Claim limited-time offers
  • Try local coffee shops, cafés, bakeries, and merchants
  • Redeem in person
