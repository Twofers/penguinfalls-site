Penguin Crash: Ice Heist

The gulls took lunch. Take it back.

Pull back, aim, and send your crew through ice, timber, and precarious fortresses. Find a weak support, start a chain reaction, and turn a well-timed shot into a very satisfying heist.

iPhone & iPad · Free

What you can do with Penguin Crash

01

Three penguins, three tricks

Hop with Skipper, slam with Chonk, or time Puff's bounce to turn an awkward landing into a clever shot.

02

An arctic adventure

Solve 30 levels across Slippery Shores, Crooked Docks, and Freezer Fortress.

03

Make every shot better

Earn completion, par, and gold medals. Play offline, retry instantly, and adjust music, effects, haptics, and reduced motion.
