Penguin Bounce: Ice Rescue

A little bounce. A big rescue.

An arctic cove, a trusty snowball, and a friend who needs a hand. Find a path through ice and rocks in an endless arcade rescue game with fresh formations every run.

iPhone · Free

What you can do with Penguin Bounce

01

Find your next rescue

New ice formations, rescue pockets, and rock layouts make each run a little different.

02

Watch the tides

Rising and falling water changes which rocks block your snowball. Time your shots as the route shifts.

03

Make it your moment

Chase your best score, pause when you need to, and choose your sound and haptic settings. No account needed.
