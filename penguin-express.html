Coffee delivery dash

Penguin Express

Special delivery. Steady flippers.

Your customers are waiting, and the ice is slippery. Guide a determined penguin through three short deliveries while protecting a very important cup of coffee. Every sip that arrives counts.

iPhone · Free

What you can do with Penguin Express

01

Swipe and slide

One swipe changes one lane. Steer around snowbanks and rough ice on the way to your customer.

02

Make every sip count

Three deliveries bring different obstacle layouts. Keep the coffee steady and aim for a perfect 300.

03

Pick up and play

Play offline without an account. Your best score stays on your device, with optional sound and haptics.
