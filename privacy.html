Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Who We Are

Penguin Falls LLC is a Texas limited liability company based in Irving, Texas. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information on the Penguin Falls LLC informational website.

This Website

This site is informational. It does not require accounts, does not sell products directly, and does not collect app account or payment information.

Email You Send Us

If you contact Penguin Falls LLC by email, we may collect your email address, the contents of your message, and any information you choose to provide. We use that information to review your inquiry, respond to you, and keep ordinary business records related to the conversation.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how many people visit this website and which pages they read. Vercel provides aggregate information such as page views, referral sources, approximate location, and browser and device type. It does not use analytics cookies or persistent cross-site identifiers.

We remove query strings and fragments from page URLs before sending them to analytics. Our website does not send app account details, email messages, or payment information to analytics. We do not load analytics when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Preview and local development visits are excluded.

Button-click reporting is not currently enabled. Read Vercel’s Web Analytics privacy information for details about its processing.

Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as needed to respond to your inquiry, operate the website, comply with law, or protect our rights, safety, or security.
