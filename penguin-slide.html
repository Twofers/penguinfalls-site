Penguin Slide: Arctic Glide

Three shots. One perfect glide.

A gentle pull or a bold launch can make all the difference. Send your penguin across the floe and land as close to the center as you can. There's always room for one more try.

iPhone · Free

What you can do with Penguin Slide

01

Simple touch controls

Pull back to choose your angle and power, then release to send your penguin gliding.

02

Three chances to shine

Land in the rings for 25, 50, or 100 points. Three perfect stops add up to 300.

03

A fresh challenge

Target positions change each run. Play offline, keep your personal best on your device, and adjust sound and haptics.
